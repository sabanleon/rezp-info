ResidentEvil ZP

Server Info

HOW IT WORKS

Humans against zombies. Every round starts with a countdown, then the T-Virus is released and some players turn into zombies.

Humans win by surviving until the timer ends. Zombies win by infecting everyone. Humans who die come back as zombies.

Every 3rd round is a special gamemode. A vote opens the round before.

Press M for the main menu.

AMMO PACKS (AP)

AP is the server money. Spend it in M > 2 Extra Items. Everyone starts with 15 AP (VIP 20).

Earn AP

Damage1 AP for every 1500 damage to zombies
Kill a zombie1 AP (Crawler 3 AP)
Infect a human1 AP
Win the round1 AP for the winning team

Round end bonus

Normal / BiohazardMost damage, most kills, most infections: 1 AP each
SwarmMost damage and most kills: 1 AP each (infections count as kills)
Boss roundsTop damage on the boss: 2 AP. The boss gets 5 AP if the zombies win, and no AP for kills
T-Virus EvolutionMost kills: 1 AP

At the end of every round you also get your own kills, damage and infections in chat.

SUPPLY DROPS

In Normal Infection rounds, a helicopter drops a supply crate 1 minute before the round ends. Everyone is warned by an alarm. The first human to reach it picks up the weapon (slot 5). It is either a Railgun or an RPG, never both in the same round. The crate glows blue for the Railgun and red for the RPG.

Railgun5 shots, no reload. The beam pierces every zombie in line (walls stop it). 800 damage, 2400 on a headshot
RPG1 rocket. 5000 damage to zombies

GAMEMODES

Every 3rd round is one of these, chosen by vote.

Normal InfectionThe main mode. About 1 in 8 players starts as a zombie. Zombies infect humans by hitting them, infected humans become zombies. Humans win if the timer ends
SwarmHalf humans, half zombies, one life. Humans die instead of being infected. Zombie claw hits make a human bleed: 2 HP every 3 seconds until a First Aid Spray. Human shop only has Flashlight Battery, First Aid Spray, Body Armor, Gas Mask, Parachute and Nightvision
BiohazardHalf humans, half zombies. Zombies kill with damage instead of infecting. Everyone who dies respawns as a zombie. Lasermines are in the shop
T-Virus EvolutionFree for all, everyone starts as a human with 1 HP and a knife. Every kill evolves you one step:
Cannibal 100 HP > Wesker 150 HP (double jump) > Verdugo 200 HP (R: enrage 3 s, every 20 s) > Nemesis 250 HP (final).
Evolved stages hit like their characters: Wesker 30 / 100, Verdugo 50 / 216, Nemesis 150 damage (left / right click). Last one alive wins. No extra items
