HOW TO GET VIP

1Join our Discord (type /discord in chat) and tell us you want VIP.
2Send your Steam ID and the length you want (10, 30 or 45 days).
3We send you the payment details. Payment is handled in Discord, never in the game.
4After payment an admin adds your VIP. It is linked to your Steam ID, so it works on any PC.
5Open M > 6 to pick your perk, knife skin and hat.

CUSTOM PREFIX

Your own tag in chat instead of the plain [VIP]. Tell us the text you want on Discord. We can refuse names that insult other people or copy an admin or another player.

PRIVATE KNIFE / HAT

A knife or hat made only for you. Nobody else can pick it.

Send us:
  • Your idea (pictures, colors, what it should look like), or a ready-made model
  • A model must be a CS 1.6 / GoldSrc .mdl with its textures
  • Models you made yourself or models you are allowed to use
We check every model before we accept it:
  • It must not be too big or heavy, so it does not hurt FPS or download time
  • Animations have to work in game
  • No offensive or copied content
  • We may ask you to change something, or we refuse the model

If you only send an idea, our modeler makes it. Making time depends on the work, we tell you when you order. Once it is ready it appears in your VIP menu.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • VIP is tied to your Steam ID. Using another account means no VIP.
  • The perk you pick stays for the whole map, even if you reconnect.
  • Questions? Ask on Discord, we answer there.
